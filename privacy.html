Kingdom Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Русская версия

This policy explains what data the Android game Kingdom Tycoon (the “game”) collects and how it is used. The game is developed by taptic (“we”).

What we collect

We do not ask for and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Through the AppMetrica analytics service (provided by Yandex LLC), the game automatically sends:

  • device identifiers: the Google advertising ID and the AppMetrica installation ID;
  • gameplay events: session start and end, tutorial progress, Keep level, number of cleared tiles, buildings, in-game currency spending, army marches, game language;
  • technical information: device model, Android version, system language, game version, crash reports and diagnostics;
  • approximate location (country and city), which AppMetrica determines from your IP address. The game does not access GPS.

Why

Only to understand how people play, where they get stuck and what breaks, and to use this to improve game balance and fix bugs. We do not sell this data and do not use it to show ads.

Where your progress is stored

Your game save is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

Who else receives the data

Analytics data is processed by AppMetrica. Yandex privacy policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential. Data is sent over an encrypted connection.

How to opt out and delete data

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings: Google → Ads.
  • To delete analytics data collected about you, email us at jerseyminsk@gmail.com. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe such data has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If the game starts collecting other data (for example, after adding ads or purchases), we will update this page and the date at the top.
