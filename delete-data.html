Kingdom Tycoon: delete your data

Developer: taptic · Privacy policy

Kingdom Tycoon has no accounts. Your game progress is stored only on your device. Analytics data (device identifiers, gameplay events, crash reports) is collected through AppMetrica.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Kingdom Tycoon and note your Android device model, or find the game in Android Settings → Apps.
  2. Send an email to jerseyminsk@gmail.com with the subject “Kingdom Tycoon data deletion”.
  3. In the email, include your device model and the approximate date you installed the game, so we can find your analytics records.
  4. We confirm by email and delete the data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • All analytics data linked to your device in AppMetrica: device identifiers, gameplay events, technical and crash data.

What is kept

  • Nothing personal. Aggregated statistics without identifiers (for example, total number of players per day) may be kept.
  • Analytics data not deleted on request is kept no longer than AppMetrica’s standard retention period.

Delete your game progress

Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Kingdom Tycoon → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your save immediately; we never receive it.
